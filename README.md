## English

This is an **unofficial, standalone Chinese build** of [Polestar](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/polestar), the free open-source leveling guide addon for World of Warcraft: Forever. It ships the **complete addon**, not a patch: **do not install it alongside the original Polestar.**

What this build adds:

- All 260 guides and 10,085 guide lines are translated, and every game name and interface string is filled in.
- Full game-name packs (quests, NPCs, objects, items, instances, encounters, factions, flight points) so guides show the names your client uses.
- Both languages load automatically on a zhCN or zhTW client; English clients are untouched.

### What Polestar does

Polestar plans leveling routes from 1 to 60 for every race and both factions, then walks you through them step by step.

- **A guide that keeps up with you**: steps tick off when the game confirms them (quests taken and handed in, kills, loot, places reached), and your place is recovered from your quest log.
- **A 3D waypoint arrow**: distances and ETAs along real walking paths, around cliffs and walls, through town gates, into caves, up and down lifts.
- **A travel planner**: flight paths, boats, zeppelins, the tram, portals, class teleports and your Hearthstone, on their real timetables.
- **Quest markers** on the map, minimap, tooltips and nameplates, plus tombstones and rare alerts.
- **Every guide in one place**: leveling, class quests, professions, reputations, mounts, pets, exploration, Legacy challenges and community guides.
- **Lookup, Dungeons and Rares**: "how do I get that?" for any item, mount, pet, NPC, quest or zone; dungeon quests, bosses and entrances; rares and treasures by zone.
- **Quality of life**: quest log helper, hearth helper, trainer reminders, gear advice, bags with junk and keep marks, XP per hour, restock, alt overview, up to six guides in tabs, gamepad support, and several skins.
- **Everything localized**: NPCs, places, items and quests named the way your game names them.

### Usage

- `/polestar` opens the guide window.
- `/polestar guides` opens the guide hub.
- `/polestar options` opens every setting.
- `/polestar welcome` shows the welcome window again.
- `/polestar help` lists every command.

## 简体中文

本包是 [Polestar](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/polestar)（魔兽世界：Forever 的免费开源练级指南插件）的**非官方完整中文构建**，包含**整个插件**而不是补丁：**不能与原版同时安装**。

本构建提供：

- 260 个指南、10,085 条指南文本全部翻译，并补齐游戏名称与界面文本。
- 完整名称包（任务、NPC、物体、物品、副本、首领、阵营、飞行点），指南显示的名称与客户端一致。
- zhCN / zhTW 客户端自动加载对应语言；英文客户端不受影响。

### Polestar 功能

Polestar 为每个种族、两个阵营规划 1 到 60 级的练级路线，并一步步带你完成。

- **跟得上你的指南**：交任务、击杀、拾取、到达地点等步骤由游戏确认后自动打勾，进度还能从任务日志恢复。
- **3D 路点箭头**：显示距离与预计时间，沿真实可行走路径绕过悬崖和城墙、穿过城门、进入洞穴、上下升降梯。
- **旅行规划**：航班、船只、飞艇、地铁、传送门、职业传送与炉石，按真实时刻表计算。
- **任务标记**：地图、小地图、提示与姓名板标记，另有墓碑与稀有警报。
- **所有指南集中一处**：练级、职业任务、专业、声望、坐骑、宠物、探索、传承挑战与社区指南。
- **查询、地下城与稀有**：任意物品、坐骑、宠物、NPC、任务、地区的获取方式；副本任务链、首领与入口；按地区列出的稀有与宝藏。
- **便利功能**：任务日志助手、炉石助手、训练师提醒、装备建议、背包垃圾/保留标记、每小时经验、补给、小号概览、最多六个指南标签页、手柄支持与多种皮肤。
- **完整本地化**：NPC、地点、物品、任务都用游戏内的中文名称。

### 用法

- `/polestar` 打开指南窗口。
- `/polestar guides` 打开指南中心。
- `/polestar options` 打开全部设置。
- `/polestar welcome` 再次显示欢迎窗口。
- `/polestar help` 列出全部命令。
